Tergel Molom-Ochir

Ph.D. Candidate, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Duke University

Research Associate Intern, Hewlett Packard Labs

tergel.molom-ochir@duke.edu · CV · Google Scholar · ORCID · GitHub

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Duke University, advised by Prof. Yiran Chen and Prof. Hai "Helen" Li. I also work with the Emerging Accelerators team at Hewlett Packard Labs.

My research develops in-memory hardware for search and reasoning. I work across emerging memory devices, content-addressable memory circuits, and architectures so that memory itself can perform search for attention, retrieval, tree search, and test-time computation.

I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Prof. Qiangfei Xia.
